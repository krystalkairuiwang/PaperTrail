Insights Analyst

Raleigh, NC · Full-time · Consumer Insights

About the role

Quill & Co. makes stationery sold in 900 stores. The Insights Analyst turns point-of-sale and survey data into the merchandising calls our buyers make each season.

Responsibilities

  • Own the weekly sell-through report and explain what moved
  • Run customer surveys and conjoint studies for new product lines
  • Build store-cluster models to guide assortment

Qualifications

  • Degree in analytics, marketing, economics, or similar
  • 1–2 years of experience; SQL and Excel required, R or Python a plus

Benefits

Medical, dental and vision coverage, a 401(k) match, and a generous employee discount.

Quill & Co. is an equal opportunity employer.
