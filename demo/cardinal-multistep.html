Atlanta, GA · Full time · Job requisition ID R-48211

About the role

Cardinal Freight moves 30,000 truckloads a week across the Southeast. The Operations Analytics team finds the slack in that network: empty miles, dwell time, and lanes we price wrong.

Responsibilities

  • Own lane-level cost and service dashboards for regional operations leaders
  • Build forecasting and optimization models for carrier capacity
  • Quantify the impact of network changes with before/after and difference-in-differences analysis

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in analytics, operations research, economics, or related field
  • 0–3 years of experience; SQL required, Python or R preferred
  • Comfort presenting to operators who don't live in spreadsheets

Cardinal Freight is an equal opportunity employer.
