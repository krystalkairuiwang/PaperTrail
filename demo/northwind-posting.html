About Northwind

Northwind Analytics helps regional retailers forecast demand and price smarter. We are a 140-person team in Durham, NC.

What you'll do

  • Build and maintain the weekly demand-forecast scorecard used by client leadership
  • Write SQL against our Snowflake warehouse and prototype models in Python
  • Turn ambiguous client questions into structured analyses and clear recommendations
  • Design pricing tests and measure lift with proper controls

Qualifications

  • Degree in business analytics, economics, statistics, or similar
  • 1–3 years in analytics or consulting
  • Strong SQL and Excel; Python or R preferred
  • Clear written and verbal communication

Compensation

$78,000 – $92,000 base, plus annual bonus and full benefits.

Northwind Analytics is an equal opportunity employer.
