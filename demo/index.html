PaperTrail demo pages

Four made-up employers, each built a different way. Open one, apply, and watch the application get logged with nothing typed into a tracker. All companies here are fictional.

Demo 1 · ATS posting with structured data
Northwind Analytics: Business Analyst
Like Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby. Submitting the form opens a separate "Thank you for applying" page.
Demo 2 · Any company careers page
Harbor & Pine Consulting: Associate Consultant, Analytics
No structured data and no known ATS. The confirmation appears in place without a new page load.
Demo 3 · Multi-step application
Cardinal Freight: Operations Analytics Associate
Workday-style steps (My Information, then Review, then Submit), with the URL changing at each step.
Demo 4 · No clear confirmation
Quill & Co.: Insights Analyst
After you submit, the site only says "All set!", so PaperTrail asks Did you just apply? instead of guessing.
These pages open as local files, so Chrome needs one setting first. Go to chrome://extensions, open PaperTrail's Details, and turn on Allow access to file URLs. Real job sites (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, and others) work without this.
